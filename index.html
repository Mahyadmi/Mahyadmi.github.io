About me

      

        

          
For almost a decade I was the go-to IT person at a heating equipment manufacturer, supporting office and production staff with 1st and 2nd line support: diagnosing hardware, software and network faults, setting up devices, supporting Windows and Microsoft 365, maintaining network infrastructure and setting up VPNs for secure remote access. I also designed and built the company's websites and web applications, after starting my career as a WordPress developer.

          
I then moved to the UK and completed an MSc in Software Engineering with Distinction at the University of Staffordshire, focusing on cloud, DevOps and modern software development. Today I work in operations at an Amazon delivery station in Stoke-on-Trent, which has shown me first-hand how much fast-moving teams depend on reliable systems.

          
I'm calm under pressure, explain technical things in plain language, and love turning messy problems into simple, working solutions.
